Our story

A NEW FIESTA IN TUSCALOOSA

Barrio Fiesta opened its doors on 14th Street as a new neighborhood gathering place just minutes from the University of Alabama campus.

The restaurant was completely renovated before opening and brings together an experienced restaurant team with familiar local hospitality.

The announcement especially highlighted longtime Tuscaloosa server Augie, whom many local families and customers already knew from other restaurants around town.

We're new — but we're already part of Tuscaloosa.
